# Swedish terms in Glimmer: where I hesitated

For Anders, to settle together with Jämmer's and Flimmer's lists (the series rule: trade terms that are said in English stay English in Swedish). Nothing here is changed until then.

Adobe's Swedish is written from memory of Photoshop's Swedish interface, not checked against a running copy; a question mark means I am not sure. For Affinity Photo and Pixelmator Pro I do not know their Swedish wording well enough to state it, so that column says so rather than guess.

| English | Glimmer today | Adobe's Swedish (from memory) | Affinity / Pixelmator | My proposal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Preset | "Preset", "Presets", "Presetfilter", but "Förinställning: %@" in one place | Förinställning | not known | **Preset** everywhere (it is what is said); change the one "Förinställning: %@" to "Preset: %@" |
| Feather | Fjädra, Fjäderradie, Maskfjädring | Ludd (Ludda) | not known | Keep Fjädra: Adobe's word is its own invention and nobody says it |
| Liquify | Fluida | Gör flytande | not known | Gör flytande, or keep English "Liquify"; "Fluida" is my coinage and the weakest on the list |
| Vibrance | Lyster | Lyster | not known | Keep |
| Dodge / Burn | Skugga / Efterbelys | Skugga / Efterbelys | not known | Keep (Adobe's words, though "Dodge"/"Burn" are also said); "Color Dodge" → Färgskugga and "Linear Dodge (Add)" → Linjär skugga (addera) follow from it |

Also seen while reading the English: the interface mixes "color" (menus: Fill with Foreground Color) and "colour" (tooltips and notes: "the foreground colour"). One spelling should be chosen; the menus follow Apple's American spelling.
