Smarter, on your Mac

  • Auto Enhance (⇧⌘L) and Auto Color (⇧⌘B) add editable adjustment layers.
  • Auto Straighten levels a tilted horizon; Suggest Crop frames the subject, including faces.
  • Select People and Select Sky; Straighten Document flattens a page or sign shot at an angle.
  • Image Text to Layers turns the words in a photo into editable text layers.
  • The omnibar suggests what the open photo needs.

Files from other programs

  • Photoshop files (PSD, PSB) open with their layers, and a document can be exported as a Photoshop file. A report says what came through.
  • Camera RAW files open as a layer that keeps the RAW file: Layer › Develop… develops it again at any time, without loss.
  • Edit › Heal Selection takes a small thing out of a photo by filling the area from around it, on your Mac.
  • Two new blend modes: Darker Color and Lighter Color.

Working with Flimmer and Jämmer

  • An image you are editing for Flimmer or Jämmer survives Quit, a crash and a restart: Glimmer keeps your edits itself after every step.
  • Quit no longer asks about such a window. Closing it asks Save Back, Keep for Later or Discard, and File › Linked Files Waiting… lists what is kept.

AI services and what they cost

  • More services to choose from in Settings › AI, for text and for pictures, each with your own key.
  • Every panel and plan that makes a picture says that the price is not known in advance, and asks once per service.
  • The Assistant shows the text model's listed price where the service states one.

Workflow

  • File › Batch Process… edits whole folders and rates photos, flagging blurry shots.
  • Export for Web with a longest side and a file-size limit.
  • AI agents can drive Glimmer through a local API: see Help › Agent Access….
  • The AI service and the model download source can be changed in Settings.

Also

  • Errors, warnings and notices are told apart by their sign, not only by their colour.
  • A new document's changes are saved a second after each step, so a sudden stop loses less.
  • A correction that changes how some documents look: in a document that blends colors in gamma space, an adjustment layer with a mask or lowered opacity now mixes the way Photoshop does.
  • For agents: a paid step also needs allowUnknownPrice; an unknown parameter is refused by name; a stopped job is canceled; answers are in English whatever language Glimmer shows.
  • A new icon, Swedish throughout, and many fixes.
